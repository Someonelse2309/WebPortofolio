Meepo : Meet Your People

Offline Emergency Mesh iOS Companion

Welcome to the official Meepo : Meet Your People support page and help center. Meepo is an offline emergency communication app for iPhone engineered for survival, disaster response, and community resilience when earthquakes, tsunamis, floods, severe storms, or power grid failures knock out cellular towers and internet access.

⚠️ Important Emergency Services Disclaimer

Meepo is a decentralized, offline peer-to-peer supplementary communication tool designed for localized post-disaster resilience when conventional networks fail.

Meepo does NOT connect to, operate, or replace official government emergency services (such as 911, 112, Basarnas, or local disaster response agencies) or satellite emergency beacons. When official telecommunication services or professional rescue channels are operational, always contact local emergency authorities first.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

English

1. General & Disaster Preparedness

Q: What is Meepo : Meet Your People?

A: Meepo is an offline communication companion for iPhone designed to keep families, neighbors, and community search teams connected when cell phone towers and power grids are destroyed or overwhelmed after a natural disaster. It connects nearby iPhones directly using Bluetooth and local Wi-Fi without relying on cellular networks or internet service.

Q: Does Meepo require cellular data, Wi-Fi, or internet access?

A: No. Meepo is built to operate 100% off-the-grid. It communicates phone-to-phone using your iPhone’s built-in wireless hardware. You do not need an active SIM card, cellular carrier service, mobile data, or internet connection.

Q: How do I create a family network or invite neighbors?

A: Follow these quick setup steps:

  1. Open Meepo, choose a 2–3 letter callsign or nickname (e.g., "NIC"), your avatar color, and a network name (e.g., "Family Safety").
  2. Tap "Create Group".
  3. Have your family members open Meepo on their iPhones and scan the on-screen QR code (or share the invite link).
  4. Once joined, everyone in your private network will immediately appear on your search radar screen.
Q: What is the difference between a Family Network and the Public Community Disaster Mesh?

A:

  • Family / Neighborhood Network (Private): Uses a private security key created on your phone. All locations, messages, and alerts are end-to-end encrypted so only invited family members can see them.
  • Public Community Disaster Mesh (Open): An open channel for seeking nearby survivors, finding volunteer aid, or coordinating with community shelters when you are separated from your family. Anyone in your immediate vicinity tuned into the public channel can communicate.
Q: How does the Search & Distance Radar (Orbit) work?

A: The radar screen shows you at the center, surrounded by concentric distance rings. Members of your network appear as colored markers showing their real-time relative distance and compass bearing. Tapping any member activates the Directional Locator / Survivor Finder, which displays a directional compass arrow pointing directly toward their last known position and shows the distance in meters or feet.

Q: How do we coordinate an Assembly / Evacuation Point?

A: Tap the map icon, select an agreed-upon safe rendezvous or evacuation shelter, select a target rendezvous time (e.g., "in 30 minutes" or "in 1 hour"), and tap Set Meeting Point. Every member of your network will receive the evacuation point on their map and radar, along with an arrival distance indicator and RSVP options (Accepted or Declined).

Q: How does emergency messaging work without cellular service?

A: Meepo includes a lightweight off-grid chat feature. You can send critical status chips (such as "I am safe", "Need medical help", or "At evacuation point") or type custom short text updates. Messages hop directly between nearby phones, and a delivery checkmark confirms when a recipient’s phone has received your message.

2. Emergency Distress Signal (SOS) & Immobility Detection

Q: How do I activate the Emergency Distress Signal (SOS)?

A: Press and hold the red SOS button for 1.5 seconds. Your iPhone will vibrate and sound a loud audible siren, and an urgent distress alert containing your latest GPS coordinate will immediately be broadcast to all members in your network.

Q: What if I trigger an SOS while trapped indoors or without a GPS signal?

A: Even if you are trapped under rubble, inside a collapsed structure, or in an area without clear sky visibility, Meepo still broadcasts the emergency distress beacon. Your network members will receive the siren alert and an emergency notification indicating that you need immediate help, even without satellite coordinates.

Q: What is the Immobility / Man-Down Alert?

A: In a disaster scenario, a survivor may be injured or unconscious. When enabled in settings, Immobility Guard monitors your iPhone’s motion sensors. If no movement is detected for your chosen duration (e.g., 3 minutes), the app sounds a 30-second warning countdown. If you do not tap to cancel the warning, Meepo automatically broadcasts an emergency SOS to your network.

Q: Can Meepo call 911, 112, or dispatch government rescue teams?

A: No. Meepo is an offline peer-to-peer radio tool that communicates only with nearby iPhones within wireless reach. It cannot place phone calls to 911, 112, or Basarnas. If cellular or landline service is available, always call local emergency authorities first.

3. Mesh Relaying & Battery Conservation

Q: What is the wireless range between devices?

A: Range depends on physical terrain and obstacles. In open outdoor areas, Bluetooth and local Wi-Fi can span 30 to 100 meters (100–300 feet). In dense urban ruins or collapsed structures, range may be reduced.

Q: What is Community Mesh Relaying?

A: In a widespread disaster, your family members might be separated by a distance greater than direct Bluetooth range. With communal relaying enabled, intermediate iPhones in your neighborhood act as relay bridges, passing encrypted packets along until they reach your family. Relaying phones cannot read or alter your messages; they simply forward the encrypted data.

Q: How does Meepo conserve battery during a prolonged blackout?

A: During an extended electrical grid failure, battery life is vital. Meepo automatically throttles GPS checks when you are stationary, and automatically disables background relaying when your phone enters Low Power Mode or drops below critical battery levels.

4. Permissions & Troubleshooting

Q: Why does Meepo need Bluetooth, Local Network, and Location permissions?

A: Because there is no cellular network, Meepo must use your iPhone's local hardware:

  • Location: Needed to calculate distances and compass bearings to family members and evacuation points.
  • Bluetooth & Local Network: Needed to discover nearby phones and exchange emergency packets without cell towers.
  • Camera: Strictly used to scan your family's invite QR code. No photos or videos are ever taken or stored.
  • Motion & Fitness: Conserves battery when you are still, and powers the optional Immobility Alert.
  • Notifications: Alerts you on your lock screen when an emergency SOS beacon is triggered.
Q: A family member is not appearing on the radar. How do we troubleshoot?

A: Check these four essential steps:

  1. Verify Radios: Ensure Bluetooth and Wi-Fi are turned ON in iOS Control Center.
  2. Verify Permissions: Open iOS Settings → Meepo and confirm Location, Bluetooth, and Local Network are enabled.
  3. Verify the Same Network: Make sure everyone scanned the same QR code or entered the same network name.
  4. Move to an Open Area: Thick concrete or metal debris can block radio signals. Step into a more open line of sight to establish the initial connection.
Q: How do I leave a network or wipe my data after an emergency?

A: Tap Settings and select "Leave Network". This immediately stops all wireless transmissions and permanently wipes all chat history, member coordinates, evacuation points, and security keys from your device.

Pusat Bantuan & Tanya Jawab (Versi Bahasa Indonesia)

Bahasa Indonesia
⚠️ Penafian Penting Layanan Darurat Resmi

Meepo adalah alat komunikasi pelengkap terdesentralisasi berbasis nirkabel lokal antarsesama (peer-to-peer) yang ditujukan untuk situasi darurat ketika jaringan telekomunikasi konvensional terputus.

Meepo TIDAK terhubung ke, mengoperasikan, atau menggantikan layanan panggilan darurat resmi pemerintah (seperti 112, Basarnas, BNPB/BPBD, atau kepolisian) maupun pemancar darurat satelit. Selalu hubungi otoritas penyelamat resmi terlebih dahulu apabila saluran telekomunikasi konvensional masih dapat diakses.

1. Bagaimana Meepo bekerja saat jaringan seluler dan internet mati total?

A: Meepo bekerja 100% luring (offline). Aplikasi ini menghubungkan iPhone langsung dengan iPhone terdekat menggunakan Bluetooth dan Wi-Fi lokal bawaan perangkat tanpa memerlukan pemancar seluler, kuota data, maupun jaringan internet.

2. Bagaimana cara menyiapkan jaringan darurat keluarga atau lingkungan?

A: Ikuti langkah mudah berikut:

  1. Buka Meepo, masukkan nama panggilan atau inisial Anda (contoh: "NIC"), pilih warna profil, dan beri nama jaringan (contoh: "Keluarga Aman").
  2. Tekan "Create Group".
  3. Minta anggota keluarga membuka Meepo di iPhone mereka dan memindai kode QR di layar Anda.
  4. Setelah bergabung, seluruh anggota keluarga akan langsung terlihat di radar pencarian!
3. Apa perbedaan Jaringan Keluarga dan Saluran Komunitas Terbuka?

A:

  • Jaringan Keluarga / Lingkungan (Privat): Menggunakan kunci enkripsi khusus keluarga Anda. Seluruh lokasi, pesan, dan titik evakuasi dienkripsi sehingga hanya keluarga Anda yang dapat melihatnya.
  • Saluran Komunitas Terbuka (Public Disaster Mesh): Saluran terbuka untuk mencari penyintas, meminta bantuan medis, atau koordinasi posko darurat saat Anda terpisah dari keluarga. Siapa pun di sekitar yang membuka saluran darurat dapat saling berkomunikasi.
4. Bagaimana cara kerja Radar Jarak & Arah (Orbit)?

A: Radar Orbit menampilkan posisi Anda di tengah, dan anggota keluarga Anda di lingkaran-lingkaran jarak di sekeliling Anda. Anda dapat menekan nama anggota keluarga untuk mengaktifkan panah penunjuk arah (Survivor Finder) yang memandu Anda menuju posisi rekan Anda secara langsung.

5. Bagaimana cara menentukan Titik Evakuasi / Posko Kumpul?

A: Buka menu peta, pilih lokasi posko aman atau tempat kumpul keluarga, tentukan target waktu (misalnya "30 menit lagi"), lalu tekan Set Meeting Point. Titik kumpul akan muncul di layar seluruh keluarga Anda lengkap dengan penunjuk jarak dan konfirmasi kehadiran (Accepted / Declined).

6. Bagaimana cara menggunakan Sinyal Darurat SOS?

A: Tekan dan tahan tombol merah SOS selama 1,5 detik. Ponsel Anda akan membunyikan sirine peringatan yang keras, bergetar, dan memancarkan sinyal darurat berprioritas tinggi beserta koordinat GPS terakhir Anda ke seluruh anggota jaringan.

7. Apa yang terjadi jika memicu SOS di dalam reruntuhan tanpa sinyal GPS?

A: Sinyal darurat SOS akan tetap dipancarkan ke ponsel keluarga Anda di sekitar. Mereka akan menerima peringatan suara sirine dan notifikasi darurat yang menyatakan bahwa Anda membutuhkan bantuan segera, meskipun koordinat satelit tidak tertangkap.

8. Apa itu Peringatan Tubuh Tak Bergerak (Man-Down Alert)?

A: Fitur keselamatan opsional ini memantau sensor gerak iPhone Anda. Jika Anda tidak bergerak dalam durasi yang ditentukan (misalnya karena tertimpa reruntuhan atau tidak sadarkan diri), aplikasi akan membunyikan peringatan hitung mundur 30 detik. Jika Anda tidak membatalkannya, aplikasi akan memancarkan sinyal darurat SOS otomatis ke keluarga Anda.

9. Apakah Meepo dapat menghubungi nomor darurat 112 atau Basarnas?

A: Tidak. Meepo hanya berkomunikasi secara lokal antarsesama ponsel iPhone dalam jangkauan nirkabel langsung. Meepo tidak dapat melakukan panggilan telepon ke 112 atau pihak berwenang. Selalu hubungi otoritas resmi terlebih dahulu jika jaringan telepon biasa masih berfungsi.

10. Bagaimana cara keluar dari jaringan dan menghapus data setelah bencana usai?

A: Buka menu Pengaturan di aplikasi lalu tekan "Leave Network" (Tinggalkan Jaringan). Semua riwayat pesan, lokasi, titik evakuasi, dan kunci keamanan akan langsung terhapus bersih dan permanen dari iPhone Anda.
