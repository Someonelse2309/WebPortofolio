Meepo : Meet Your People

Offline Emergency Mesh Disaster Relief Companion Zero-Cloud Architecture

Welcome to the official Meepo : Meet Your People support page and help center. Meepo is an offline communication app for iPhone engineered for disaster relief posts, emergency response teams, volunteers, and community resilience when earthquakes, tsunamis, floods, severe storms, or power grid failures knock out cellular towers and internet access.

Important Emergency Services Disclaimer

Meepo is a decentralized, offline peer-to-peer supplementary communication tool designed for localized post-disaster resilience when conventional networks fail.

Meepo does NOT connect to, operate, or replace official government emergency services (such as 911, 112, Basarnas, BNPB/BPBD, police, or medical dispatch) or satellite emergency beacons. When official telecommunication services or professional rescue channels are operational, always contact local emergency authorities first.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

English

1. General & Disaster Preparedness

Q: What is Meepo : Meet Your People?

A: Meepo is an offline communication companion for iPhone designed to keep disaster relief-post coordinators, volunteers, and affected community members connected when cellular towers and power grids are destroyed or overwhelmed. It connects nearby iPhones directly using Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) and local Wi-Fi (Bonjour TCP) without relying on cellular carriers, mobile data, SIM cards, or internet access.

Q: Does Meepo require cellular data, external Wi-Fi routers, or internet access?

A: No. Meepo is built to operate 100% off-the-grid. It communicates phone-to-phone using your iPhone’s built-in wireless hardware. You do not need an active SIM card, cellular carrier service, mobile data plan, or internet connection.

Q: How do I create a relief-post group or invite participants?

A: Follow these quick setup steps:

  1. Open Meepo, enter a 2–3 letter callsign or nickname (e.g., "NIC"), choose your profile color, and enter a group name (e.g., "Tent A Relief").
  2. Tap "Create Group" to display your group's unique invite QR code.
  3. Have other participants open Meepo on their iPhones, select "Join Group", and scan the QR code with their camera (or paste an invite link meepo://join#...).
  4. Review and accept the community mesh agreement. Once joined, everyone in your private group will immediately appear on the Orbit radar screen.
Q: How is our communication secured across the mesh?

A: When a group is created, a unique 256-bit symmetric encryption key is generated directly on the iPhone. All group payloads—including GPS coordinates, status messages, meeting points, and emergency SOS alerts—are end-to-end encrypted using ChaCha20-Poly1305 authenticated encryption. When sharing the group via QR code or invite link, the key is packed inside the URL fragment (#), which is never sent across HTTP or to any server. Only devices that scanned or joined your specific group can decrypt and read your updates.

Q: How does the Search & Distance Radar (Orbit) work?

A: The Orbit radar screen displays you at the center, surrounded by concentric distance rings (zoomable from 5 m to 120 m). Group members appear as colored markers showing their real-time relative distance, callsign initials, received heading pointer, and battery status when critical (≤ 10%). Tapping any member or selecting them from the Friend List activates Follow Mode, which displays a directional compass guide, straight-line distance, and a live progress track. Overlapping members cluster into +N badges, while members beyond the outer ring project as perimeter directional glows.

Q: How do we coordinate an Assembly / Meeting Point?

A: Tap the map icon, place a rendezvous pin on an agreed-upon safe shelter or supply station, select a target time preset (e.g., Now, in 15 min, in 30 min, or in 1 hour), and tap Set Meeting Point. All members of your group immediately receive the rendezvous pin on their map and radar, along with distance calculation, ETA, and RSVP options (Accepted or Declined). Automatic arrival detection triggers when a member comes within 25 meters.

Q: How does emergency messaging (Chat) work without cellular service?

A: Meepo includes an off-grid chat feature for concise updates (up to 20 UTF-8 bytes). You can tap one-touch status chips (such as "I need help", "Where are you?", "I'm safe", "At the shelter", "I've arrived", or "On my way") or type custom short text. Messages hop directly between nearby phones or through intermediate mesh relays. A delivery checkmark confirms when a recipient device acknowledges (ACK) the packet. Messages are stored strictly in volatile RAM (up to 50 messages) and are never written to permanent disk.

Q: Does Meepo support offline maps?

A: Yes. Meepo features an offline vector map capability that covers a 15 km radius around your disaster relief post or city (~100 MB safe tile pack). This allows coordinators and volunteers to view streets, terrain, and meeting points completely without an active internet or cellular connection.

2. Emergency Distress Signal (SOS) & Inactivity Detection

Q: How do I activate the Emergency Distress Signal (SOS)?

A: Swipe up on the red SOS button (upward swipe gesture). Your iPhone will vibrate and sound an urgent audible siren, immediately broadcasting a prioritized emergency distress packet with your latest GPS coordinates across the local mesh network.

Q: How do receiving devices handle an incoming SOS, and how is it ended?

A: Receiving iPhones sound a loud audible siren and haptic pattern repeating every 2 seconds, displaying an alert banner with two actions: "Dismiss" and "I'll be there!". Tapping either action silences the urgent 2-second siren and transitions to 90-second reminders while maintaining a pulsing red edge glow. Tapping "I'll be there!" sends an immediate acknowledgment back to the sender (showing a notice that help is coming) and initiates Follow Mode on the radar. When the emergency is resolved, the sender taps "End SOS" on their persistent banner, broadcasting an authenticated resolution packet (0x08) that clears the alert and sirens on all receiving devices.

Q: What if I trigger an SOS while trapped indoors or without a GPS signal?

A: Even if you are trapped under rubble or inside a collapsed concrete structure without clear sky visibility, Meepo still broadcasts the emergency distress beacon (using an epoch fallback with 255 m uncertainty flag). Your group members will receive the siren alert, Lock Screen critical notification, and emergency banner indicating that you need immediate assistance, even without satellite coordinates.

Q: What is the Inactivity Guard / Man-Down Alert?

A: In a disaster scenario, a rescuer or survivor may become injured, entrapped, or unconscious. When enabled in settings, Inactivity Guard monitors your iPhone's accelerometer for physical stillness (variance < 0.15g). If no movement is detected for 3 minutes (180 seconds), the app initiates a 30-second silent/haptic warning countdown. If you do not tap to cancel the warning, Meepo automatically broadcasts an emergency SOS to your group.

Q: Can Meepo call 911, 112, or dispatch government rescue teams?

A: No. Meepo is an offline peer-to-peer supplementary radio tool that communicates strictly with nearby iPhones within wireless reach. It cannot place phone calls to 911, 112, Basarnas, or government dispatch centers. If cellular or landline service is available, always call local emergency authorities first.

3. Mesh Relaying & Battery Conservation

Q: What is the wireless range between devices?

A: Direct peer-to-peer radio range between iPhones using Bluetooth Low Energy and local Wi-Fi is typically 30 to 100 meters (100–300 feet) in open outdoor areas. In dense debris or collapsed buildings, direct range may be reduced. Mesh relaying overcomes these limits by forwarding packets hop-by-hop across chains of participating phones (illustrative spacing ≈30 m per hop).

Q: What is Community Mesh Relaying and the Carry Store?

A: In a disaster relief post, volunteers at supply stations and coordinators may be separated by distances greater than direct Bluetooth range. With communal relaying, intermediate iPhones act as bridges, passing encrypted 56-byte packets along until they reach the recipient (up to 8 hops for standard packets, 15 hops for SOS). Relaying phones forward raw ciphertext blindly without the ability to read message contents. If a recipient is temporarily out of range, the Carry Message Store holds encrypted chat, ACK, and SOS resolution packets in temporary device memory for up to 600 seconds (10 minutes) before evicting them.

Q: How does Meepo conserve battery during a prolonged electrical blackout?

A: During an extended power blackout, conserving battery is critical:

  • Adaptive GPS Sampling: GPS publishes updates every 2 seconds when moving with distant peers, but throttles down to 20 seconds when stationary, and 60 seconds in Survival Mode.
  • Automatic Battery Protection: Relay participation automatically pauses when your battery reaches 20% or lower, or when iOS Low Power Mode is activated.
  • Rate Limiting: Forwarding is capped (8 ordinary packets/sec, 4 SOS packets/sec) to avoid overheating and radio drain.

4. Permissions & Troubleshooting

Q: Why does Meepo need Bluetooth, Local Network, Location, Camera, Motion, and Notification permissions?

A: Because there is no cellular or internet infrastructure, Meepo relies on on-device hardware:

  • Location: Shows your position when selecting a meeting point and shares relative distance/bearing with your group on the Orbit radar.
  • Bluetooth: Discovers nearby iPhones and exchanges encrypted mesh packets without internet.
  • Local Network: Discovers nearby phones over local Wi-Fi (Bonjour TCP) for reliable shelter-wide communication.
  • Camera: Strictly used to preview and scan group invite QR codes. No photos or videos are ever recorded or stored.
  • Motion & Fitness: Adjusts location update cadence based on physical movement and monitors stillness for Inactivity Guard.
  • Notifications & Live Activities: Sounds critical emergency siren alerts on your Lock Screen and powers Dynamic Island / Lock Screen Live Activities during an active session.
Q: A team member is not appearing on the radar. How do we troubleshoot?

A: Check these essential troubleshooting steps:

  1. Verify Radios: Ensure Bluetooth and Wi-Fi are switched ON in iOS Control Center.
  2. Verify Permissions: Open iOS Settings → Meepo and confirm Location, Bluetooth, and Local Network permissions are granted.
  3. Verify Same Group: Confirm that all members scanned the same invite QR code or joined using the same invite link.
  4. Check Contact Status: Orbit marks members stale after 30 seconds of inactivity and hides them after 90 seconds without contact (unless an active link or SOS is present). Moving closer or reconnecting restores visibility.
  5. Clear Line of Sight: Thick reinforced concrete or metal debris can attenuate radio signals. Move into a more open area to establish the initial connection.
Q: How do I leave a group or wipe my data after coordination ends?

A: Tap Settings in the app and select "Leave Group". This immediately halts all radio broadcasts, closes peer connections, clears the carry store, and permanently purges all in-memory chat history, active peer locations, meeting points, and cryptographic keys from your device.

Bahasa Indonesia
Penafian Penting Layanan Darurat Resmi

Meepo adalah alat komunikasi pelengkap terdesentralisasi berbasis nirkabel lokal antarsesama (peer-to-peer) yang ditujukan untuk situasi darurat pascabencana ketika jaringan telekomunikasi konvensional lumpuh atau terputus.

Meepo TIDAK terhubung ke, mengoperasikan, atau menggantikan layanan panggilan darurat resmi pemerintah (seperti 112, Basarnas, BNPB/BPBD, kepolisian, atau ambulans) maupun pemancar darurat satelit. Selalu hubungi otoritas penyelamat resmi terlebih dahulu apabila saluran telekomunikasi konvensional masih dapat diakses.

1. Bagaimana Meepo bekerja saat jaringan seluler dan internet mati total?

A: Meepo beroperasi 100% luring (offline). Aplikasi ini menghubungkan iPhone langsung dengan iPhone terdekat menggunakan Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) dan Wi-Fi lokal bawaan perangkat (Bonjour TCP) tanpa memerlukan pemancar seluler, kartu SIM, kuota data, maupun koneksi internet.

2. Bagaimana cara menyiapkan grup darurat posko atau keluarga?

A: Ikuti langkah mudah berikut:

  1. Buka Meepo, masukkan inisial panggilan 2–3 huruf Anda (contoh: "NIC"), pilih warna profil, dan beri nama grup (contoh: "Posko Tenda A").
  2. Tekan "Create Group" untuk menampilkan kode QR undangan grup Anda.
  3. Minta anggota tim atau keluarga membuka Meepo di iPhone mereka, pilih "Join Group", lalu pindai kode QR di layar Anda (atau tempel tautan undangan meepo://join#...).
  4. Setelah menyetujui ketentuan jaringan, seluruh anggota akan langsung tampil di radar Orbit secara bersamaan!
3. Bagaimana keamanan dan enkripsi komunikasi di Meepo?

A: Saat grup dibuat, kunci simetris 256-bit dibuat langsung di iPhone Anda. Seluruh transmisi (lokasi GPS, pesan obrolan, titik kumpul, dan sinyal SOS) dienkripsi ujung-ke-ujung menggunakan algoritma ChaCha20-Poly1305. Saat membagikan kode QR atau tautan, kunci tersebut diletakkan di fragmen URL (#) yang tidak pernah dikirimkan ke peladen web luar. Hanya rekan yang memindai atau bergabung ke grup Anda yang dapat membuka dan membaca pesan Anda.

4. Bagaimana cara kerja Radar Jarak & Arah (Orbit)?

A: Radar Orbit menampilkan posisi Anda di tengah, dikelilingi lingkaran-lingkaran jarak dinamis (skala zoom 5 m hingga 120 m). Rekan di grup tampak sebagai penanda berwarna dengan inisial nama, indikator arah kompas relatif, serta indikator baterai lemah jika ≤ 10%. Menekan salah satu rekan akan mengaktifkan Mode Ikuti (Follow Mode) yang menampilkan panduan arah kompas, jarak garis lurus, dan lintasan kemajuan. Rekan yang berada di luar lingkaran terluar diproyeksikan sebagai kilau penunjuk arah di pinggiran radar.

5. Bagaimana cara menentukan Titik Evakuasi / Posko Kumpul (Meeting Point)?

A: Buka menu peta, letakkan pin lokasi posko aman atau tempat pembagian bantuan, pilih target waktu kumpul (opsi: Sekarang, 15 menit lagi, 30 menit lagi, atau 1 jam lagi), lalu tekan Set Meeting Point. Titik kumpul akan muncul di layar peta dan radar seluruh anggota grup lengkap dengan penunjuk jarak, perkiraan waktu tiba, serta konfirmasi kehadiran (Accepted atau Declined). Deteksi tiba otomatis aktif jika anggota berada dalam radius 25 meter.

6. Apakah Meepo mendukung peta luring (Offline Map)?

A: Ya. Meepo dilengkapi fitur paket peta vektor luring dengan cakupan radius 15 km di sekitar posko bencana atau kota Anda (~100 MB). Ini memungkinkan Anda melihat jalan, kontur geografis, dan titik evakuasi tanpa memerlukan koneksi internet sama sekali.

7. Bagaimana cara mengirim pesan obrolan darurat luring?

A: Meepo menyediakan fitur obrolan luring ringan untuk pembaruan cepat (hingga 20 byte UTF-8). Anda dapat menekan tombol status cepat (seperti "I need help", "Where are you?", "I'm safe", "At the shelter", "I've arrived", atau "On my way") atau mengetik pesan teks pendek. Tanda centang konfirmasi (ACK) akan muncul saat perangkat penerima telah menerima pesan. Pesan hanya disimpan di RAM sementara (maksimal 50 pesan) dan tidak pernah disimpan di memori permanen.

8. Bagaimana cara memicu Sinyal Darurat SOS dan menyelesaikannya?

A: Usap ke atas (swipe up) pada tombol merah SOS. Ponsel Anda akan membunyikan sirine peringatan yang keras, bergetar, dan memancarkan paket darurat berprioritas tinggi beserta koordinat GPS terakhir Anda ke seluruh grup.
Ponsel penerima akan membunyikan sirine tiap 2 detik dengan opsi "Dismiss" atau "I'll be there!". Menekan salah satunya meredakan sirine darurat ke pengingat tiap 90 detik. Menekan "I'll be there!" langsung mengaktifkan Mode Ikuti di radar dan memberi tahu pengirim bahwa bantuan sedang menuju ke lokasi.
Saat keadaan darurat selesai, pengirim menekan tombol "End SOS" pada spanduk aktif untuk menyiarkan paket resolusi (0x08) yang mematikan sirine dan peringatan di seluruh ponsel penerima.

9. Apa yang terjadi jika memicu SOS di dalam reruntuhan tanpa sinyal GPS?

A: Sinyal darurat SOS akan tetap dipancarkan ke ponsel rekan di sekitar melalui gelombang radio lokal meskipun satelit GPS terhalang reruntuhan. Anggota grup Anda tetap akan menerima sirine darurat, notifikasi layar kunci, dan spanduk peringatan bahwa Anda membutuhkan pertolongan segera.

10. Apa itu Peringatan Tubuh Tak Bergerak (Inactivity Guard / Man-Down Alert)?

A: Fitur keselamatan opsional ini memanfaatkan sensor gerak (akselerometer) iPhone. Jika ponsel tidak mendeteksi gerakan sama sekali selama 3 menit (180 detik)—misalnya karena pingsan atau tertimpa puing—aplikasi akan menjalankan hitung mundur peringatan haptik 30 detik. Jika Anda tidak membatalkannya, Meepo secara otomatis menyiarkan sinyal darurat SOS ke grup Anda.

11. Apakah Meepo dapat menghubungi nomor darurat 112 atau Basarnas?

A: Tidak. Meepo hanya berkomunikasi secara lokal antarsesama ponsel iPhone dalam jangkauan nirkabel radio langsung. Meepo tidak dapat melakukan panggilan telepon seluler ke 112 atau Basarnas. Selalu hubungi otoritas resmi terlebih dahulu jika jaringan telepon biasa masih berfungsi.

12. Bagaimana cara kerja Estafet Pesan Komunal (Mesh Relaying) dan proteksi baterai?

A: Jika penerima berada di luar jangkauan radio langsung (jarak normal ≈30 meter per lompatan), iPhone perantara akan meneruskan paket data tersandi secara estafet (hingga 8 lompatan untuk paket biasa dan 15 lompatan untuk SOS). Ponsel perantara tidak dapat membaca isi data tersebut. Jika koneksi terputus sementara, fitur Carry Store akan menyimpan paket di RAM hingga 10 menit (600 detik). Untuk melindungi daya perangkat Anda, fitur relai otomatis dijeda saat baterai mencapai 20% ke bawah atau saat Mode Daya Rendah (Low Power Mode) diaktifkan.

13. Anggota tim tidak muncul di radar. Bagaimana cara mengatasinya?

A: Periksa lima hal penting berikut:

  1. Pastikan Radio Aktif: Pastikan Bluetooth dan Wi-Fi dalam posisi AKTIF di Pusat Kontrol iOS.
  2. Periksa Izin: Buka Pengaturan iOS → Meepo dan pastikan izin Lokasi, Bluetooth, dan Jaringan Lokal (Local Network) telah diberikan.
  3. Grup yang Sama: Pastikan semua rekan memindai kode QR grup yang sama.
  4. Batas Waktu Kontak: Radar menandai rekan tidak aktif setelah 30 detik dan menyembunyikannya setelah 90 detik tanpa kontak (kecuali ada sinyal SOS aktif). Mendekat kembali atau menyambung ulang akan menampilkan mereka kembali.
  5. Bebas Rintangan: Puing beton tebal atau logam dapat meredam sinyal radio. Bergeserlah ke lokasi yang lebih terbuka untuk menyambungkan koneksi awal.
14. Bagaimana cara keluar dari grup dan menghapus data setelah bencana usai?

A: Buka menu Pengaturan di aplikasi lalu tekan "Leave Group" (Tinggalkan Grup). Semua riwayat pesan sementara, lokasi rekan, titik evakuasi, antrean pesan, dan kunci keamanan akan langsung terhapus bersih dan permanen dari memori iPhone Anda.
