Important Emergency Services Disclaimer

Meepo is a decentralized, offline peer-to-peer supplementary communication tool designed for localized post-disaster resilience when conventional telecommunication networks fail.

Meepo does NOT connect to, operate, or replace official government emergency services (such as 911, 112, Basarnas, BNPB/BPBD, police, or medical emergency dispatch) or satellite emergency beacons. When official telecommunication services or professional rescue channels are operational, always contact local emergency authorities first.

Zero Cloud Storage

We operate zero central servers or cloud databases. Relief-post coordinates, chat updates, and emergency alerts never leave your local physical vicinity.

No Accounts Required

No names, emails, passwords, phone numbers, or government IDs required. You coordinate using only a local 2–3 letter callsign and profile color.

Zero Trackers or Ads

No third-party analytics libraries, advertising identifiers (IDFA), commercial trackers, or telemetry tracking SDKs are embedded in the app.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Under Apple’s App Store Privacy Nutrition standards, Meepo qualifies under the "Data Not Collected" category:

  • No User Accounts or Registration: You are never asked for your real name, email address, phone number, password, or identity documents. There is no central account creation.
  • No Central Servers or Remote Cloud: We do not operate, connect to, or maintain remote servers, cloud storage, or backends. Your group communications stay strictly within your direct or multi-hop radio reach.
  • No Third-Party Analytics or Tracking SDKs: We do not include third-party analytics libraries, advertising trackers, or crash reporting SDKs that send device telemetry to external corporations.
  • No Advertising Identifiers (IDFA): The App contains zero commercial advertisements and never tracks your activity across other apps or websites.
  • No Remote Location Surveillance: Geographic coordinates are processed entirely on-device and are never transmitted to or logged by any central database. Your coordinates are shared only with authenticated participants in your active encrypted group.

2. How Meepo Operates Off-The-Grid

Meepo is engineered to keep relief-post coordinators, volunteers, and affected community members connected during severe telecom blackouts:

  • Direct Device-to-Device Wireless Mesh: Meepo establishes direct peer-to-peer connections between iPhones using built-in Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) and local Wi-Fi (Bonjour TCP _teekum._tcp), operating 100% independently of cellular carriers, SIM cards, internet service providers, and electrical grid infrastructure.
  • End-to-End Encrypted Private Relief-Post Groups: When a coordinator creates a group, Meepo generates a cryptographically secure 256-bit symmetric key directly on the device. All payloads—including GPS coordinates, meeting points, chat messages, and emergency SOS alerts—are sealed using ChaCha20-Poly1305 authenticated encryption. Only group participants possessing the shared key can decrypt and read transmissions.
  • Serverless Key Exchange via URL Fragment (#): When sharing a group via QR code or invite link (meepo://join#...), the 256-bit symmetric key is placed strictly inside the URL fragment (#). Because web browsers and HTTP proxies never transmit the fragment portion across the internet, group keys cannot leak to external web servers even if shared through text.
  • Multi-Hop Community Relaying: When group members are separated by distances exceeding a single radio link (illustrative spacing ≈30 m per hop) or blocked by rubble, intermediate iPhones can forward encrypted 56-byte wire packets hop-by-hop (up to 8 hops for standard packets and 15 hops for SOS). Relaying devices handle raw ciphertext blindly: they cannot decrypt, inspect, or tamper with message contents.
  • Battery-Aware Relay Protection: Relaying automatically pauses when device battery falls to 20% or lower, or when iOS Low Power Mode is enabled, safeguarding your device's battery life for personal survival. Forwarding is also rate-limited (8 ordinary packets/sec, 4 SOS packets/sec) to prevent channel congestion.
  • Temporary Store-and-Forward ("Carry Store"): If an intended recipient is momentarily out of radio reach, encrypted chat, acknowledgment (ACK), and SOS resolution packets can be held in temporary device memory for up to 600 seconds (10 minutes) before being permanently evicted.

3. What Is Stored on Your Device

Meepo stores only the absolute minimum operational data locally on your iPhone:

  • Local Profile & Preferences: You select a 2–3 letter callsign/initials (e.g., "NIC"), a group name (e.g., "Tent A Relief"), and an avatar color. These preferences are saved in local app storage so you can quickly restore your session during an ongoing emergency.
  • Hardware-Secured Keychain Keys: Active group symmetric keys are stored in your iPhone's hardware-secured Keychain (kSecAttrAccessibleAfterFirstUnlockThisDeviceOnly). They are never synced to iCloud or backed up. To prevent packet replay and cryptographic nonce reuse across restarts, a durable packet sequence counter record is also maintained in the Keychain without storing group secrets.
  • Ephemeral In-Memory Chat History: Chat messages (up to 20 UTF-8 bytes per message) are held strictly in volatile RAM (capped at 50 messages). They are never written to permanent disk storage and are permanently erased upon leaving the group or closing the app.
  • Temporary Operational Data: Live peer coordinates, radar bearings, meeting points, and carry store packets exist solely in volatile device RAM during an active session and are wiped immediately upon exit.

5. Emergency Distress Signal (SOS) & Inactivity Guard

  • Emergency Distress Signal (SOS): Swiping up on the red SOS button immediately triggers an audible siren and haptic alert on your device and broadcasts an urgent distress packet with your latest GPS coordinates (or last known location) to all members of your group.
  • SOS Resolution ("End SOS"): While an outgoing SOS is active, a persistent banner offers the "End SOS" action. Tapping it broadcasts an authenticated resolution packet (0x08) that clears the emergency alert, silences sirens, and extinguishes the red edge glow on all receiving devices.
  • Receiver Response & Follow Mode ("I'll be there!"): When receiving an SOS, devices sound a repeating siren every 2 seconds. Members can tap "Dismiss" or "I'll be there!" to silence the immediate alarm and transition to 90-second reminders. Tapping "I'll be there!" immediately activates Follow Mode on the radar and notifies the sender that help is on the way.
  • Inactivity Guard / Man-Down Alert (Optional): If enabled in settings, Meepo uses your iPhone's accelerometer to detect physical stillness (motion variance < 0.15g) for 3 minutes (180 seconds). If stillness is detected, a 30-second silent/haptic pre-alarm warning countdown is triggered. If not cancelled, Meepo automatically broadcasts an emergency SOS to your group. Motion sensor evaluation occurs 100% on-device and is never recorded.

6. Data Deletion & Exiting a Group

Leaving a Group: Tapping "Leave Group" in Settings immediately halts all radio transmissions, closes peer connections, clears the carry store, and permanently purges all active member coordinates, meeting points, ephemeral chat messages, and group encryption keys from device memory.
Deleting the App: Uninstalling Meepo from your iPhone completely and irreversibly removes all local settings, saved callsigns, and Keychain cryptographic keys.

7. Children's & Community Safety Privacy

Because Meepo collects zero personal information, requires no accounts, and operates without cloud infrastructure, it is fully safe for volunteers, students, children, and relief teams coordinating during disaster emergencies.

8. Legal Compliance & User Rights

In full accordance with global privacy legislation—including the Indonesian Personal Data Protection Law (Undang-Undang No. 27 Tahun 2022 tentang Pelindungan Data Pribadi / UU PDP), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)—you retain absolute sovereignty and ownership over your data. Because all operational data resides solely on your physical hardware, you maintain total physical control over its generation and deletion at all times.

1. Informasi yang Tidak Kami Kumpulkan

Sesuai dengan standar Perlindungan Privasi Apple (App Store Privacy Nutrition Labels), Meepo tergolong dalam kategori "Data Not Collected" (Data Tidak Dikumpulkan):

  • Tanpa Akun Pengguna atau Registrasi: Anda tidak perlu mendaftarkan nama lengkap, email, nomor telepon, kata sandi, maupun dokumen identitas kependudukan. Tidak ada pembuatan akun.
  • Tanpa Peladen Pusat atau Cloud: Kami tidak memiliki, mengoperasikan, atau menyimpan data di peladen internet (cloud). Koordinat lokasi, pesan obrolan, dan sinyal darurat hanya berada di lingkup jangkauan radio nirkabel lokal Anda.
  • Tanpa Analitik atau Pelacak Pihak Ketiga: Kami tidak menyematkan pustaka analitik pihak ketiga, pelacak iklan, atau perekam diagnostik yang mengirimkan data perangkat Anda ke pihak luar.
  • Tanpa Pengidentifikasi Iklan (IDFA): Aplikasi bebas dari iklan komersial dan tidak pernah melacak aktivitas Anda di aplikasi lain.
  • Tanpa Pengawasan Lokasi Terpusat: Titik koordinat GPS tidak pernah dicatat di peladen pusat mana pun. Lokasi Anda hanya dibagikan secara lokal kepada rekan di grup aktif yang memiliki kunci enkripsi yang sama.

2. Cara Kerja Meepo Tanpa Jaringan Seluler (Off-The-Grid)

Meepo dirancang untuk membantu koordinator posko bantuan, relawan, dan penyintas tetap terkoordinasi di tengah pemadaman telekomunikasi total:

  • Jaringan Nirkabel Langsung Antar-Perangkat (Mesh): Meepo menghubungkan iPhone secara langsung menggunakan Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) dan Wi-Fi lokal bawaan perangkat (Bonjour TCP _teekum._tcp), tanpa ketergantungan pada menara seluler, kartu SIM, penyedia internet, maupun jaringan listrik PLN.
  • Grup Posko Terenkripsi Ujung-ke-Ujung (End-to-End): Saat grup dibuat, kunci kriptografi simetris 256-bit dibuat langsung di iPhone Anda. Seluruh transmisi—termasuk koordinat GPS, titik kumpul (meeting point), pesan obrolan, dan sinyal SOS—dienkripsi secara aman dengan algoritma ChaCha20-Poly1305. Hanya rekan yang bergabung di grup Anda yang dapat membaca dan melihatnya.
  • Pertukaran Kunci Tanpa Peladen melalui Fragmen URL (#): Tautan undangan dan kode QR grup (meepo://join#...) menyimpan kunci 256-bit di dalam bagian fragmen URL (#). Karena peramban web tidak pernah mengirimkan fragmen URL ke peladen internet, kunci grup tidak dapat bocor ke luar meskipun tautan dibagikan.
  • Estafet Pesan Komunal (Multi-Hop Relay): Di area posko yang luas atau terhalang puing reruntuhan (jarak antar-perangkat ≈30 m per lompatan), iPhone perantara dapat membantu meneruskan paket data tersandi 56-byte secara estafet (hingga 8 lompatan untuk paket biasa dan 15 lompatan untuk SOS). Ponsel perantara hanya meneruskan data terenkripsi tanpa dapat membuka atau membaca isinya.
  • Proteksi Baterai Otomatis: Fitur relai otomatis dijeda saat daya baterai iPhone mencapai 20% ke bawah atau saat Mode Daya Rendah (Low Power Mode) aktif. Laju pengiriman juga dibatasi (8 paket biasa/detik, 4 paket SOS/detik) untuk menjaga kestabilan perangkat.
  • Penyimpanan Pesan Sementara (Carry Store): Paket pesan teks, konfirmasi (ACK), dan resolusi SOS dapat disimpan sementara di memori perangkat hingga 600 detik (10 menit) untuk diteruskan saat rekan kembali masuk ke jangkauan radio, setelah itu akan dihapus permanen.

3. Data yang Tersimpan di Perangkat Anda

Meepo hanya menyimpan data minimal langsung di memori lokal iPhone Anda:

  • Profil & Preferensi Lokal: Inisial panggilan 2–3 huruf (contoh: "NIC"), nama grup (contoh: "Posko Tenda A"), dan warna profil. Data ini disimpan di penyimpanan lokal perangkat agar Anda dapat langsung memulihkan sesi koordinasi darurat.
  • Kunci Enkripsi di Rantai Kunci (Keychain): Kunci grup tersimpan di Rantai Kunci (Keychain) perangkat yang terlindungi secara perangkat keras (kSecAttrAccessibleAfterFirstUnlockThisDeviceOnly). Kunci tidak pernah dicadangkan ke iCloud. Penghitung urutan paket (sequence counter) juga disimpan di Keychain untuk mencegah serangan pemutaran ulang (replay attack).
  • Riwayat Obrolan Sementara di RAM: Pesan obrolan singkat (maksimal 20 byte UTF-8) hanya disimpan di memori sementara (RAM) dengan batas maksimal 50 pesan. Pesan tidak pernah disimpan ke penyimpanan permanen dan langsung terhapus saat keluar dari grup atau menutup aplikasi.
  • Data Operasional Sementara: Posisi GPS rekan, arah kompas radar, titik kumpul evakuasi, dan paket estafet hanya berada di RAM selama sesi berlangsung dan dihapus bersih saat sesi berakhir.

5. Sinyal Darurat SOS & Peringatan Tubuh Tak Bergerak

Sinyal Darurat SOS: Mengusap tombol SOS ke atas (swipe up) seketika membunyikan sirine dan getaran di ponsel Anda serta memancarkan paket darurat berprioritas tinggi beserta koordinat GPS terakhir Anda ke seluruh anggota grup.
Penyelesaian SOS ("End SOS"): Pengirim memiliki spanduk darurat dengan tombol "End SOS". Menekannya akan menyiarkan paket resolusi terotentikasi (0x08) yang menghentikan sirine dan mematikan pendar merah di seluruh ponsel penerima.
Respons Penerima & Mode Ikuti ("I'll be there!"): Penerima mendengar sirine berulang tiap 2 detik dengan opsi "Dismiss" atau "I'll be there!". Memilih salah satunya meredakan sirine darurat ke pengingat 90 detik. Memilih "I'll be there!" langsung menyalakan Mode Ikuti (Follow Mode) di radar dan memberi tahu pengirim bahwa bantuan sedang menuju ke lokasi.
Peringatan Tubuh Tak Bergerak (Inactivity Guard - Opsional): Jika diaktifkan, sensor gerak memantau apabila Anda tidak bergerak sama sekali selama 3 menit (180 detik). Aplikasi menampilkan hitung mundur peringatan haptik 30 detik sebelum otomatis menyiarkan sinyal SOS ke grup. Pemrosesan sensor gerak berjalan 100% di perangkat.

6. Penghapusan Data & Keluar dari Grup

Keluar dari Grup: Menekan tombol "Leave Group" (Tinggalkan Grup) di Pengaturan seketika menghentikan transmisi radio, menutup koneksi nirkabel, membersihkan antrean estafet, dan menghapus seluruh pesan obrolan sementara, titik kumpul, lokasi rekan, dan kunci enkripsi dari memori perangkat.
Menghapus Aplikasi: Menghapus Meepo dari iPhone akan memusnahkan seluruh data pengaturan, inisial, dan kunci Keychain yang tersimpan secara tuntas dan permanen.
