When official telecom services or professional rescue channels are operational, always contact local emergency authorities first.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Under Apple’s App Privacy standards, Meepo qualifies under the "Data Not Collected" category:

  • No User Accounts or Registration: You are never asked for your full name, email address, phone number, password, or government ID. There is no account registration.
  • No Central Servers or Remote Cloud: We do not maintain any servers or cloud infrastructure. Your survival coordinates, messages, and alerts never leave your local physical vicinity.
  • No Third-Party Analytics or Tracking SDKs: We do not include third-party analytics libraries, advertising trackers, or crash reporting tools that send device diagnostics to outside corporations.
  • No Advertising Identifiers (IDFA): The App contains no advertisements and never tracks your activity across other apps or websites.
  • No Remote Location Surveillance: Your geographic coordinates are never logged by us or transmitted to any central database. Your location is visible only to people in your immediate wireless range who share your network.

2. How Meepo Operates Off-The-Grid

Meepo is engineered to keep families, neighbors, and community search-and-rescue teams connected during critical telecom blackouts:

  • Direct Device-to-Device Wireless Mesh: The App connects iPhones directly using built-in Bluetooth and local Wi-Fi, completely independent of cellular carriers, internet service providers, or power grid infrastructure.
  • End-to-End Encrypted Family & Neighborhood Networks: When you create a private network, a unique cryptographic security key is created directly on your iPhone. All communications within your private network—including GPS coordinates, status messages, assembly points, and emergency distress alerts—are securely encrypted. Only family and group members who joined your specific network can read what you send.
  • Public Community Disaster Mesh: If you are separated from your family or seeking nearby survivors and community aid, you can connect to the open "Public Community Disaster Mesh". In this mode, messages and status updates are shared openly with any nearby device tuned into the public disaster channel.
  • Community Mesh Relaying: In widespread disaster zones, survivors may be separated by collapsed structures or distances exceeding direct Bluetooth range. If you opt into communal relaying, your iPhone acts as a cooperative relay node, passing encrypted messages along to other nearby phones. Your phone cannot open or read messages belonging to other groups—it simply extends the radio reach for your community. Relaying automatically suspends when your battery runs low.
  • Temporary Store-and-Forward ("Carry Store"): If an intended recipient is temporarily out of radio range, encrypted status messages can be carried in your phone's temporary memory for up to 10 minutes until their phone comes back into range, after which expired messages are erased.

3. What Is Stored on Your Device

Meepo stores only the bare minimum operational data locally on your iPhone:

  • Local Identity & Network Setup: You choose a short 2–3 letter callsign or nickname (e.g., "NIC"), a network name (e.g., "Family Safety"), and an avatar color. These preferences are saved locally on your iPhone so you can immediately resume communication during an ongoing emergency.
  • Secure Network Keys: Private network keys are generated on-device and stored in your iPhone's hardware-secured Keychain. They are never synced to iCloud or backed up to any cloud service.
  • Temporary In-Memory Information: Live survivor positions, radar bearings, emergency status messages, and evacuation points exist only in temporary device memory while the session is active. All temporary information is permanently wiped as soon as you leave the network or close the App.

4. Device Permissions & Why We Need Them

To function off-the-grid, Meepo requests access to specific iPhone hardware features. You can review or adjust these permissions at any time in iOS Settings → Meepo:

Permission Why Meepo Needs It in a Disaster Scenario

5. Emergency Distress Signal (SOS) & Immobility Alert

  • Emergency Distress Beacon (SOS): Holding the SOS button for 1.5 seconds triggers a loud audible siren on your phone and broadcasts an urgent emergency packet with your latest GPS coordinates to everyone in your network.
  • Immobility / Man-Down Alert (Optional): If you enable this feature in settings, the App uses your iPhone's motion sensors to detect whether you have remained motionless for an extended duration (which may indicate entrapment or injury). If no movement is detected, the App sounds a 30-second warning countdown and can automatically broadcast an emergency SOS to your network if you are unable to respond. Motion sensor data is evaluated entirely on-device and is never recorded or saved.

6. Data Deletion & Exiting a Network

Leaving a Network: Tapping "Leave Network" immediately halts all radio transmissions and permanently erases all active messages, evacuation points, member coordinates, and private encryption keys from your device.
Deleting the App: Uninstalling Meepo completely and irreversibly purges all local settings, saved preferences, and security keys from your iPhone.

7. Children's & Family Safety Privacy

Because Meepo collects zero personal data, maintains no user accounts, and operates without external servers, it is completely safe for children, students, and families coordinating during natural disasters.

8. Legal Compliance & User Rights

In accordance with global privacy laws—including the Indonesian Personal Data Protection Law (Undang-Undang No. 27 Tahun 2022 tentang Pelindungan Data Pribadi / UU PDP), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)—you have absolute ownership and control over your data. Because all data remains exclusively on your physical device, you can view, modify, or permanently delete your data at any second directly through the App.

⚠️ Penafian Penting Layanan Darurat Resmi

Meepo adalah alat komunikasi pelengkap terdesentralisasi berbasis nirkabel lokal antarsesama (peer-to-peer) yang ditujukan untuk situasi darurat ketika jaringan telekomunikasi konvensional terputus.

Meepo TIDAK terhubung ke, mengoperasikan, atau menggantikan layanan panggilan darurat resmi pemerintah (seperti 112, Basarnas, BNPB/BPBD, atau kepolisian) maupun pemancar darurat satelit. Selalu hubungi otoritas penyelamat resmi terlebih dahulu apabila saluran telekomunikasi konvensional masih dapat diakses.

1. Informasi yang Tidak Kami Kumpulkan

Sesuai dengan standar Perlindungan Privasi Apple (App Store Privacy Nutrition Labels), Meepo tergolong dalam kategori "Data Not Collected" (Data Tidak Dikumpulkan):

  • Tanpa Akun Pengguna atau Registrasi: Anda tidak perlu mendaftarkan nama lengkap, email, nomor telepon, kata sandi, maupun dokumen identitas apa pun. Tidak ada pembuatan akun.
  • Tanpa Peladen Pusat atau Cloud: Kami tidak memiliki atau mengoperasikan peladen internet. Titik koordinat, pesan status, dan sinyal darurat Anda tidak pernah dikirim ke komputer jarak jauh.
  • Tanpa Analitik atau Pelacak Pihak Ketiga: Kami tidak menyematkan pustaka analitik pihak ketiga, pelacak iklan, atau perekam diagnostik yang mengirimkan data perangkat Anda ke perusahaan luar.
  • Tanpa Pengidentifikasi Iklan (IDFA): Aplikasi tidak memuat iklan komersial dan tidak pernah melacak aktivitas Anda di aplikasi lain.
  • Tanpa Pengawasan Lokasi Terpusat: Titik koordinat lokasi Anda tidak pernah dicatat oleh kami dan tidak disimpan di basis data pusat mana pun. Lokasi Anda hanya dapat dilihat oleh rekan yang berada di jangkauan radio lokal jaringan aktif Anda.

2. Cara Kerja Meepo Tanpa Jaringan Seluler (Off-The-Grid)

Meepo dirancang untuk menjaga keluarga, warga lingkungan, dan tim pencarian warga tetap terhubung di tengah pemadaman telekomunikasi total:

  • Jaringan Nirkabel Langsung Antar-Perangkat (Mesh): Aplikasi menghubungkan iPhone secara langsung menggunakan Bluetooth dan Wi-Fi lokal bawaan perangkat, tanpa ketergantungan pada menara seluler, penyedia internet, maupun jaringan listrik PLN.
  • Jaringan Keluarga & Lingkungan Terenkripsi Aman: Saat Anda membuat jaringan privat, kunci keamanan kriptografi unik dibuat langsung di iPhone Anda. Seluruh komunikasi di dalam jaringan privat—termasuk koordinat GPS, pesan status, titik evakuasi, dan sinyal SOS—dienkripsi secara aman. Hanya anggota keluarga yang bergabung di jaringan Anda yang dapat membaca dan melihatnya.
  • Saluran Komunitas Terbuka (Public Disaster Mesh): Apabila Anda terpisah dari keluarga atau mencari penyintas dan bantuan warga di sekitar, Anda dapat terhubung ke saluran darurat komunitas terbuka. Pesan status dapat dilihat oleh perangkat lain di sekitar yang memantau saluran bencana tersebut.
  • Bantuan Estafet Pesan Komunal (Relay): Di area bencana yang luas, penyintas mungkin terhalang puing bangunan atau berada di luar jangkauan langsung Bluetooth. Jika Anda menyetujui fitur relai, iPhone Anda dapat membantu meneruskan pesan tersandi milik penyintas lain. Ponsel Anda tidak dapat membaca isi pesan tersebut. Fitur relai otomatis nonaktif saat baterai ponsel melemah.
  • Penyimpanan Pesan Sementara: Pesan status dapat disimpan sementara di memori ponsel hingga 10 menit untuk diteruskan saat teman kembali mendekat ke jangkauan radio, setelah itu pesan akan dihapus permanen.

3. Data yang Tersimpan di Perangkat Anda

Meepo hanya menyimpan data minimal langsung di iPhone Anda:

  • Nama Panggilan & Jaringan Lokal: Anda memilih inisial 2–3 huruf (contoh: "NIC"), nama jaringan (contoh: "Keluarga Aman"), dan warna profil. Data ini disimpan di iPhone Anda agar Anda dapat langsung melanjutkan koordinasi saat situasi darurat berlangsung.
  • Kunci Keamanan di Rantai Kunci (Keychain): Kunci jaringan privat dibuat di perangkat dan disimpan di Rantai Kunci (Keychain) iOS yang aman. Kunci ini tidak pernah diunggah ke internet atau disinkronkan ke cloud.
  • Data Sementara di Memori: Lokasi penyintas, arah radar, pesan status darurat, dan titik evakuasi hanya berada di memori sementara saat aplikasi digunakan secara aktif. Data ini langsung terhapus bersih saat Anda keluar dari jaringan atau menutup aplikasi.

5. Sinyal Darurat SOS & Peringatan Tubuh Tak Bergerak

Sinyal Darurat SOS: Menekan dan menahan tombol SOS selama 1,5 detik akan membunyikan sirine di ponsel Anda dan memancarkan sinyal darurat berprioritas tinggi beserta koordinat GPS terakhir Anda ke seluruh anggota jaringan.
Peringatan Tubuh Tak Bergerak (Man-Down Alert - Opsional): Jika diaktifkan, sensor iPhone akan memantau jika Anda tidak bergerak dalam kurun waktu tertentu. Jika tidak ada gerakan, aplikasi menampilkan hitung mundur peringatan 30 detik dan dapat mengirim sinyal SOS otomatis ke keluarga Anda jika Anda tidak merespons.

6. Penghapusan Data & Keluar dari Jaringan

Keluar dari Jaringan: Menekan tombol "Tinggalkan Jaringan" (Leave Network) seketika menghentikan pemancaran radio dan menghapus seluruh pesan, titik evakuasi, lokasi rekan, dan kunci keamanan jaringan dari perangkat Anda.
Menghapus Aplikasi: Menghapus aplikasi Meepo dari iPhone akan memusnahkan seluruh data pengaturan dan kunci yang tersimpan secara tuntas dan permanen.

7. Hak Pengguna & Kepatuhan Hukum (UU PDP)

Sesuai dengan Undang-Undang Republik Indonesia No. 27 Tahun 2022 tentang Pelindungan Data Pribadi (UU PDP) serta standar GDPR internasional, Anda memiliki kendali fisik mutlak atas data Anda. Karena seluruh data berada di perangkat Anda sendiri dan tidak pernah ada di peladen kami, Anda dapat memperbarui atau menghapus seluruh data kapan saja secara mandiri melalui aplikasi.
